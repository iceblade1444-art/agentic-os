# Welcome to your Agentic OS vault

This folder is an **Obsidian vault** exposed to agents through the `obsidian` MCP server.
Hermes and the built-in orchestrator can `list_notes`, `read_note`, `search_notes`,
`create_note`, and `append_note` here as part of a mission.

## Ideas
- Draft a weekly research digest from web-search results
- Keep a running log of completed missions
- Summarize long documents into linked notes

> Point `OBSIDIAN_VAULT` at your real vault to use these tools on your own notes.
